About India

India is a large country in South Asia. More than 1.4 billion people live here. They speak many languages, follow many religions, and celebrate many festivals — yet they all belong to one nation.

This idea is called Unity in Diversity. It means we can be different and still live together with respect.

“The beauty of India is that so many cultures live side by side, like colours in a rainbow.”

Festivals of India

Festivals bring families and neighbourhoods together. Here are some of the most loved ones:

Diwali

The festival of lights. People decorate homes with diyas, share sweets, and remember the victory of good over evil.

Holi

The festival of colours. Friends play with gulal, sing, and welcome spring with joy.

Eid

A festival of gratitude. Families pray, wear new clothes, and share food with neighbours and the needy.

Pongal & Baisakhi

Harvest festivals. Farmers thank nature for crops, and people cook special dishes together.

Christmas

Celebrated with carols, stars, and cakes. In India it is enjoyed by people of many communities.

Independence Day

On 15 August we remember 1947, hoist the Tiranga, and feel proud of our freedom.

Food from Every Region

Indian food changes from state to state. Spices like turmeric, cumin, and cardamom make it famous all over the world.

Region Popular dishes
North India Butter chicken, chole bhature, naan, lassi
South India Dosa, idli, sambar, filter coffee
East India Rosogolla, fish curry, momos in the hills
West India Dhokla, pav bhaji, vada pav, seafood

Dance, Music and Clothes

India has classical dances such as Bharatanatyam, Kathak, Odissi, and Kathakali. Folk dances like Bhangra, Garba, and Ghoomar are full of energy.

Classical music has two main styles: Hindustani in the north and Carnatic in the south. Instruments include the sitar, tabla, veena, and flute.

Traditional clothes are also part of culture: saree, salwar-kameez, lehenga, dhoti, kurta, and turban. People often wear them on festivals and weddings.

Famous Monuments

Taj Mahal

In Agra. Shah Jahan built this white marble tomb for Mumtaz Mahal. It is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Red Fort

In Delhi. The Prime Minister hoists the flag here on Independence Day.

Qutub Minar

A tall minaret in Delhi, more than 800 years old.

Gateway of India

A grand arch in Mumbai, facing the Arabian Sea.

Fun Facts for Class 7

  • India has 22 official languages in the Constitution, and hundreds of other languages and dialects.
  • The national animal is the tiger, the national bird is the peacock, and the national flower is the lotus.
  • The Ashoka Chakra in the flag has 24 spokes, standing for duty through the day.
  • Yoga and Ayurveda began in India many centuries ago.
  • The world’s largest written constitution is the Constitution of India.
  • India is home to many religions: Hinduism, Islam, Christianity, Sikhism, Buddhism, Jainism, and more.
